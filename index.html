Privacy Policy — DoomsDayClient

Application: DoomsDayClient (Discord application ID 1233146574109016234)

Last updated: 12/08/2026

Contact: doomsddayclient@gmail.com — also reachable by opening a support ticket on our Discord server.

DoomsDayClient (“the bot”) is a private Discord application operated by the administration team of a single Discord community. It is not a public bot: it is not listed in any bot directory and cannot be invited to other servers. This policy describes exactly what data the bot accesses, what it stores, why, and how you can have it removed.

1. Who operates the bot

DoomsDayClient is operated by the administration team of the Discord server it runs on (“we”, “us”). The bot runs on infrastructure controlled by us. We are the data controller for the data described below.

2. Privileged Gateway Intents we use

Discord requires us to disclose which privileged intents the bot uses and why.

Server Members Intent

What we access: join and leave events for members of our server, the server’s invite use-counts, and the member list at the moment an administrator runs the /leaderboard command.

Why: (a) to automatically close and archive a member’s open support tickets when that member leaves the server; (b) to determine which invite a joining member used, so we can show administrators how many members each person has invited; (c) to list every member holding a support-team or beta-tester role in an internal staff activity leaderboard.

What we store: the ID of the server, the user ID of a member who created an invite, and an integer count of how many people that invite brought in. For support tickets, we store the user ID of the member who opened the ticket. We do not store member lists, usernames, avatars, nicknames, role assignments, e-mail addresses or any other profile data.

Message Content Intent

What we access: the text of messages sent in channels on our server that the bot can see.

Why: (a) automated moderation — detecting and removing external links, Discord server invite links (advertising), unauthorised file attachments, and a recurring “check my bio” scam pattern used to funnel members to malicious links; (b) spam detection — comparing a member’s recent messages to identify flooding, which results in a temporary Discord timeout; (c) recognising legacy ! prefix commands and their arguments; (d) generating a transcript of a support ticket when that ticket is closed.

What we store: only transcripts of closed support ticket channels — see section 3. Message content from ordinary public channels is examined in memory at the moment the message is sent and is then discarded. It is never written to disk. The short-term message history used for spam detection lives only in memory, covers a rolling window of sixty seconds, and is not persisted.

Presence Intent

Not used. The bot does not request or process presence, status, activity, game, Rich Presence, Spotify or custom-status data.

3. Support ticket transcripts

When you open a support ticket, the bot creates a private channel between you and our support team. When that ticket is closed, the conversation in that channel is saved as a transcript.

  • Transcripts cover only the ticket channel you yourself opened. We never store the contents of public channels, of direct messages, or of conversations you are not part of.
  • A transcript contains the messages in the ticket, their authors’ user IDs and usernames, timestamps, and links to any attachments posted in the ticket.
  • Transcripts are stored in a database on our own server. They are readable only through a web panel that requires logging in with Discord and holding a designated staff role.
  • We keep transcripts so the support team can review how a case was handled and refer back to it. They are not published, not shared with third parties, and not sold.

You do not have to have your messages stored: opening a ticket is entirely voluntary. If you do not want a transcript to exist, do not open a ticket, or ask us to delete it (section 6).

5. Sharing and automated processing

  • We do not sell, rent, or share any of this data with third parties.
  • We do not use message content, or any other data collected through the bot, to train, fine-tune or evaluate machine learning or AI models, and we do not send it to any third-party AI service.
  • Access is limited to the server’s administration and support team through a role-gated panel.

6. Your rights — access and deletion

You can ask us at any time to:

  • tell you what data we hold about you,
  • delete your ticket transcripts,
  • delete your invite counts and activity statistics.

Contact us at REPLACE_WITH_EMAIL or by opening a support ticket on the server. We action deletion requests within 30 days. Leaving the server does not automatically delete existing transcripts — ask us if you want them removed.

If you are in the EU/EEA, you have rights under the GDPR including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, and the right to lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority (in Poland: Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych).

7. Retention

Ticket transcripts and ticket records are retained for as long as the community operates, or until deletion is requested. Spam-detection message history is retained for sixty seconds in memory. Invite counts and activity statistics are retained until deletion is requested or the counters are reset by an administrator.

8. Security

Data is held on infrastructure we control, behind a web panel that requires Discord OAuth authentication and a designated staff role. Access is limited to the administration team.

9. Children

The bot is used on a Discord server subject to Discord’s own Terms of Service, which require users to meet Discord’s minimum age requirement. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone below that age.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the most recent version. Material changes will be announced on the server.
